ABOUT QUICKSHIP

Delivery that keeps business moving.

QuickShip is a proposed last-mile delivery service for e-commerce sellers, retailers, small businesses and individuals. The concept focuses on convenient pickup, clear status updates and cost-aware delivery planning.

◎Target customersE-commerce, SMEs, retailers and individuals
⌖Service areaMajor Indian cities and selected nearby routes
✦Value propositionConvenience, visibility and predictable pricing
